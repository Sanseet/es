// 07 FAQ

QUESTIONS?

We have got answers.

Which editing software does EditorsSpace support?+
We currently support Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, and Avid Media Composer. Our plugin architecture means new NLEs are added regularly based on community demand.
Is my footage secure in the cloud?+
Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption, SOC 2 Type II compliance, and zero-knowledge architecture. Your raw footage never leaves your local machine unless you explicitly choose to sync it.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?+
Yes, no questions asked. You can cancel anytime from your account settings. If you cancel, you will retain access until the end of your billing period.
Do you offer educational discounts?+
Yes! Students and educators get 50% off Pro plans. Contact our support team with your .edu email or institutional ID to get set up.
How does billing work for teams?+
Studio plans bill per seat with volume discounts starting at 10 seats. You can add or remove editors mid-cycle and we prorate automatically — no need to contact support.
What happens to my projects if I downgrade?+
Your projects and version history stay intact. You will just be limited to the active project count and export resolution of your new plan going forward.
